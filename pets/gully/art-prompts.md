# Gully Seagull Character Art Prompts - Full Long Beach Vision

## Character: "Good Golly He's Gully"

**The Complete Vision:** Gully is an imaginary friend from Long Beach, NY, born in 1984 alongside his human companion. He started as invisible, but through the collective belief of a crew of 90s kids at block parties, he became real. He's a Drop Dead Fred for the xennial generation - chaotic, protective, slightly dangerous, but ultimately loyal. He's seen everything from handball court dominance to block party revelations. He wears sunglasses because he's seen too much, drinks rum and coke because he's earned it, and smokes because the 90s were a different time.

**Essential Elements (Non-Negotiable):**
- Black Wayfarer sunglasses (pushed down to reveal bloodshot eyes when needed)
- Thick gold chain with illuminati pyramid eye pendant (symbol of seeing what others can't)
- Rum and coke with visible ice cubes (the drink of champions)
- Joint with visible smoke wisps (authentic 90s burnout culture)
- Slightly disheveled feathers (lived-in, party-animal aesthetic)
- Confident smirk like he knows secrets about Long Beach
- Beach bum aesthetic but with depth and history

## MASTER Character Prompt (DALL-E 3 - Maximum Detail)

```
A 16-bit pixel art character sprite of an anthropomorphic seagull named "Good Golly He's Gully." He stands upright like a human but has a white seagull body with light grey wing-arms, orange beak, and orange webbed feet. He wears black Wayfarer-style sunglasses that are slightly pushed down on his nose, revealing bloodshot red eyes underneath. Around his neck is a thick gold chain with a prominent pyramid pendant that has an eye in the center (illuminati symbol). In his right wing-hand he holds a rocks glass with ice cubes and amber liquid (rum and coke). A lit joint is visible in his left wing-hand with thin grey smoke wisps curling upward. His feathers are slightly messy and disheveled, like he's been going to Long Beach block parties since the 90s. He has a confident, knowing smirk on his face - the expression of someone who has seen everything this beach town has to offer. The style should be detailed 16-bit pixel art like classic SNES/Genesis games but with modern polish. Bold black outlines, vibrant but slightly desaturated colors (like faded 90s photographs), proper shading for depth. Character should be centered and take up 80% of the frame. Background is solid bright green (#00FF00) for chroma key. NO text, NO logos, NO watermarks. Focus on making the sunglasses, gold chain, and drink clearly readable at small sizes. This character should feel like he belongs at a 1990s Long Beach block party but also exists outside of time.
```

## Technical Specifications (Non-Negotiable)

- **Grid:** 8 columns × 9 rows
- **Cell size:** 192×208 pixels each
- **Total size:** 1536×1872 pixels
- **Background:** Chroma key green (#00FF00)
- **Transparency:** Unused cells (rows 5-8) must be fully transparent
- **Consistency:** Same character proportions across ALL frames
- **Alignment:** Character centered in each cell
- **Detail level:** High enough to read at 64-128px
- **Style:** 16-bit pixel art with 90s faded color palette
